# CreatorClips

CreatorClips is a Mac app for Paid Creators members. It turns your long videos into short, captioned clips, edits your raw recordings into finished vertical reels, and helps you remix and react to other videos. Your videos are made on your own computer, and the AI steps use your own OpenRouter key.

## What you need

- A Mac with **Apple silicon** (M1 or newer) on **macOS 14 Sonoma or newer**, or an **Intel** Mac on **macOS 15 Sequoia or newer**.
- An [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/keys) API key with credit. You pay OpenRouter for what you use. Each paid run shows its estimated cost before it starts, and **Settings → Spending** in the app shows what your runs cost.

## Download and install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/trentrichards34/creatorclips-releases/releases/latest).
2. Under **Download**, pick the file for your Mac:
   - Apple silicon: `CreatorClips-X.Y.Z-mac-arm64.dmg`
   - Intel: `CreatorClips-X.Y.Z-mac-x64.dmg`

   Not sure which Mac you have? Open the Apple menu → **About This Mac**. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple silicon; "Processor: … Intel" means Intel.
3. Open the downloaded `.dmg` and drag **CreatorClips** into your **Applications** folder.
4. Open CreatorClips from Applications, then follow the setup steps to add your OpenRouter key.

The app is signed with our Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## Updates

CreatorClips updates itself. It checks for a new version shortly after it opens and every few hours, downloads it in the background, and installs it when you choose **Restart to update** or the next time you quit. You can also choose **Help → Check for Updates…**.

Updates only work when CreatorClips is in your Applications folder. If it's running from the disk image, **Settings → About** offers to move it there.

To see what changed, open **Help → Changelog** in the app, or read the notes on each [release](https://github.com/trentrichards34/creatorclips-releases/releases).
