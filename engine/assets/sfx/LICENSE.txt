CreatorClips built-in sound effects

Members can use every sound in this folder in any video, including paid and monetized
posts, without attribution or royalties. Each file's source and license is listed in
manifest.json ("source", "license"). There are two kinds:

1. Recorded sounds by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released into the public domain under
   Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   "This content is free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects."
   Crediting Kenney is not required; we do it here with thanks.

   Packs used (files copied from kapishdima/soundcn, an MIT-licensed repository that keeps
   Kenney's files unchanged in its assets/kenney_* folders; the packs' own License.txt,
   where soundcn kept one, states CC0):
     - Interface Sounds (1.0)   -> pop/, tick/, click/
     - Casino Audio             -> swipe/, paper/card-place-*, pop/chip-lay-3, tick/chip-tap
     - Impact Sounds            -> impact/ (a heavy punch layered with a soft heavy impact)
     - RPG Audio                -> paper/book-*
   Kenney publishes all of its game assets under CC0 (www.kenney.nl/support, "License").

   License check (2026-10-23). soundcn kept the pack's own License.txt only for Interface
   Sounds (and Digital Audio, Voiceover Pack Fighter, not used here); its text reads
   "License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0) http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
   This content is free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects." For the
   three packs without one, what was found:
     - soundcn's own registry (public/r/*.json) lists every one of its 813 Kenney sounds,
       Casino, Impact and RPG included, as "author": "Kenney <https://kenney.nl>",
       "license": "CC0".
     - Kenney's own uploads on OpenGameArt list CC0: "54 Casino sound effects (cards, dice,
       chips)" (kenney_casino-audio.zip), https://opengameart.org/content/54-casino-sound-effects-cards-dice-chips,
       and "50 RPG sound effects" (RPGsounds_Kenney.zip),
       https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects.
     - The Godot Asset Library lists all three as CC0, as mirrored on GitHub by Loppansson:
       kenney-casino-audio-for-godot (asset 7721), kenney-impact-sounds-for-godot (7723),
       kenney-rpg-audio-for-godot (7725). Those repositories could not be opened from here, so
       the packs' License.txt text itself was not read for Casino, Impact and RPG.
   Every source found says CC0; no sound was changed. To close it out, download the three
   packs from www.kenney.nl (casino-audio, impact-sounds, rpg-audio) and keep their
   License.txt files next to this one.

   Nothing from soundcn's "wow" folder is used: those sounds belong to Blizzard
   Entertainment and are not free to use.

2. Air sounds made for CreatorClips from shaped random noise by scripts/sfx/build_library.py
   (whoosh/air-*, riser/air-rise-*). No samples, recordings or tones. Copyright (c) 2026
   Wealthery, under the engine's MIT license (../../LICENSE), with the same permission to
   use them in any video as above.

Processing: every file was trimmed of silence, peak-normalized to -3 dBFS and encoded as
48 kHz mono Ogg Opus; the impacts are two Kenney sounds mixed together.
