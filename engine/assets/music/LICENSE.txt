CreatorClips built-in background music

calm-pad.ogg, upbeat-pulse.ogg, lofi-beat.ogg and cinematic-swell.ogg are original
works made for CreatorClips by scripts/music/generate_tracks.py. Every sound in them
is synthesized from plain math (sine waves, sums of sine waves and shaped noise from
fixed random seeds). No samples, recordings, loops or other third-party audio were
used, and no melody or arrangement was copied from an existing work.
