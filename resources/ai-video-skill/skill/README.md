# AI Video Editing Skill

Make ready-to-post 1080×1920 reels from one prompt. Claude writes the script (or uses yours), voices it, animates every scene in a motion-design style that's proven on Instagram, checks its own frames, and exports an mp4.

- **3 styles**: `classic` (paper + accent colour, kinetic captions, your own cartoon presenter), `explainer3d` (3D "how it actually works" explainers), `voxel` (warm toy-world diorama with little people).
- **Any niche, any language.** Fonts for other scripts download on demand.
- **Your topic, your script, your angle.** It handles the hook, pacing, captions, timing and sound design.
- Renders on your own computer. No subscriptions beyond your AI plan and ElevenLabs.

Start with `SETUP.md`. Then just ask: *"Make a reel about ..."*.

What's inside: `SKILL.md` (the workflow Claude follows), `guides/` (scripting, direction, self-review, presenter, delivery, style guides), `engines/` (the renderers), `library/` (real scenes from viral reels to reuse), `tools/`, `playbook/brand-deals.md` (bonus).
